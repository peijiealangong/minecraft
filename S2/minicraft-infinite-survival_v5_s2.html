MINICRAFT 3D

Explore a procedurally generated, endlessly expanding block world. Mine deep caves, discover ore veins, craft tools, build a home, meet villagers, and survive the night.

W A S D — Move · Space — Jump
Mouse — Look around
Left click — Mine / Attack
Right click — Place / Hold to eat
E — Inventory & crafting
1–9 / Wheel — Hotbar selection
Shift — Sprint · F — Fly toggle
K / L — Save / Load world

Inventory & Crafting

Hotbar — click a slot to select it
Backpack — click an item to place it in the selected hotbar slot

Food: select an apple or meat in the hotbar, then hold right mouse button to eat. Tools automatically improve mining speed and unlock tougher ores.

Crafting recipes

Craft planks from logs, sticks from planks, and tools from sticks and materials. Some recipes require a crafting table or furnace.
